Today every section header and every KPI label is uppercase mono at 0.16em tracking — roughly 40 of them per screen. Used everywhere it stops being an accent and becomes the body voice, and all-caps measurably slows scanning. Keep mono for tickers, ISINs, timestamps, FX stamps, and broker names. Everything else goes sentence case.
